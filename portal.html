Help

Questions students ask

A lecture says "Video unavailable" or "This video is private"

You're signed in to a different Google account. Tap your profile picture on YouTube, switch to the Gmail address you enrolled with, and open the link again. Still stuck? Message me on WhatsApp.

Can I watch on my phone?

Yes. Open the lecture link in the YouTube app while signed in with your enrolled Gmail. It works on phones, tablets and laptops.

Can I download the lectures?

No. Lectures are streamed only, so you can rewatch them any time while signed in, but they can't be downloaded.

Can I share my access with a friend?

No. Access is linked to your own Google account, so a shared link won't play for anyone else.

How long do I keep access?

Access details for your course are confirmed on WhatsApp when you enrol.

Fair use: lectures are for your personal study only. Recording, re-uploading or sharing them is not allowed and leads to access being removed.
