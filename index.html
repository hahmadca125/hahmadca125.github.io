My CFA story

How I passed all three levels while working

Third attempt. Persistence pays.

Learn

Write your own notes during lectures, then revise them every weekend so nothing builds up.

Practise

Once the syllabus is done, work through question banks and mock exams, and learn from every mistake.

Peak

In the final month, study 8–10 hours a day in focused sessions with proper breaks, good food and rest.
